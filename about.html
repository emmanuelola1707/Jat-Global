Who We Are

Real Estate, Made Easier To Navigate

JAT Global Property Limited is a real estate developer and real estate agent based in Lagos, Nigeria. We work with clients looking to acquire land, homes and investment plots across Lagos and its neighbouring growth corridors.

Our role is to make the property process clearer — presenting each opportunity with straightforward information on pricing, documentation and features, and staying available to answer questions directly as clients decide.

This website is a digital presentation of the properties and opportunities we currently market, built to make it easier for prospective buyers to browse, ask questions and take the next step.
